— Our story

One printer in a spare bedroom. Now a family workshop.

I'm Juan Pablo, a U.S. veteran. Luma-Isla began in 2019 with a single 3D printer and a quiet ambition — to build something with my own hands that I could one day pass down.

Word of mouth turned one printer into two. Two into five. Today we run thirty-plus printers out of our shop in Oklahoma. Every piece that leaves here is hand-sanded, primed, painted, bronze-finished, or detailed by hand.

— Our process

From printer to finished piece

Every sculpture passes through four hands-on stages before it ships.

i

Print

Premium PETG, layer by layer, on machines we've maintained and tuned over six years.

ii

Sand & Prime

Each piece is hand-sanded to soften every line, then primed for a true finish.

iii

Finish

Hand-painted, bronze-washed, or detailed depending on the piece. No two are identical.

iv

Inspect & Ship

Personally inspected by family before packing. Memorials ship discreetly.
