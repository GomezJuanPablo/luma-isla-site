Catholic & Multi-Faith Statues

— 03 · Keepsakes

Pet Memorials & Urns

Designed as quiet, lasting keepsakes for families who have lost a beloved companion. Each memorial is built with a removable head and a full interior compartment sized to hold your pet's complete ashes — the piece reads as a sculpture from every angle, with the urn function completely concealed.

Many buyers also tuck their pet's collar, ID tag, or a small handwritten note inside alongside the ashes. It becomes a complete, dignified urn disguised as art — not a token keepsake compartment.

Currently in catalog: Doberman, Guardian Angel memorial. Custom breed-specific work from your photos: $149–$249 depending on size and detail.

Coming soon: Golden Retriever, Labrador, English Bulldog, French Bulldog, German Shepherd, generic Cat memorial.

— 04 · Fandom

Cosplay Masks & Collectibles

Hand-painted masks and life-size fandom busts built with real convention wear and display-quality finishing in mind. Vented for airflow, under 1 lb, with a fitting guide for custom interior padding so the mask sits right for your head and face.

Currently in catalog: Black Panther triple-mask set (T'Challa, Killmonger, M'Baku), M'Baku wearable mask, Venom life-size bust, Treebeard Ent bust.

Custom sizing: Send three measurements (head circumference, ear-to-ear, forehead-to-nape) and we'll size to your face. Adds about 5 business days.

Every fan-inspired piece is independently designed and sold as an original art piece. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any studio, publisher, or licensor.

— 05 · Trades

Milwaukee Packout Accessories

Durable 3D-printed jobsite accessories made in the USA for people who actually use their tools. Compatible with the Milwaukee Packout system — independent design, no affiliation with Milwaukee Tool.

Material: Carbon-fiber-reinforced ABS (ABS-CF). Jobsite-grade heat resistance for sealed truck beds in summer, significantly higher strength than standard 3D-printing filaments, and confirmed load capacity up to 300 lbs when properly mounted.

Custom colors & branding: Red, black, yellow, and custom shop colors are routine. Volume pricing on orders of 10+ units. Crew branding available — popular with construction crews, electrical contractors, plumbers, and HVAC teams.
