— Custom orders

Made for you, by hand.

If the piece you're imagining isn't in the catalog yet, we can probably make it.

Message the workshop

Custom commissions are some of our favorite work. They take 2–4 weeks depending on complexity, and we always confirm a target ship date in writing before any work starts. Message us with your idea and a target budget — we'll quote within a few hours and confirm the timeline before any commitment.

— What we make custom

Common commission types.

— Pet memorials

From your photographs

Send 3–5 clear photos, the pet's name, and any dates to engrave. Breed-specific work runs $149–$249 depending on size and detail. Optional sealed compartment for ashes.

— Religious & sacred

Saints & figures not yet in catalog

Catholic, Buddhist, Hindu, Jewish, Islamic, Orthodox Christian, Sikh, Bahá'í, and indigenous spiritual figures — all available as commissions. Approached with the respect they deserve.

— Cosplay

Fitted to your measurements

Custom masks in custom paint schemes, sized to your head circumference, ear-to-ear, and forehead-to-nape measurements. Custom sizing adds about 5 business days.

— Wildlife

Non-standard sizes & finishes

Larger statement pieces, smaller desk versions, matched scaling across a set, or unusual finishes (antique copper, weathered stone, raw matte black). All possible.

— Memorial

Human loved ones & milestones

Commemorative pieces for the milestones that matter — graduations, retirements, anniversaries, the memory of a loved one. Hand-engraved names and dates available.

— Trades & crews

Branded Packout accessories

Your shop colors, logo, and design printed in ABS-CF. Volume pricing on 10+ units. Popular with construction, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC crews.

— Finishes & personalization

Any piece, your finish.

For nearly every statue and bust we offer, you can request a non-standard finish at no extra charge in most cases:

  • Bronze (default)
  • Antique copper
  • Weathered stone (ivory)
  • Raw matte black

Hand-engraving: Catholic statues +$10 (perfect for First Communion, Confirmation, baptism). Pet memorials +$12 (name and dates). Cosplay sets — custom paint pattern and head-sizing included.

Message before ordering to request engraving and confirm the exact spelling.

— How to start

Tell us what you want to make.
