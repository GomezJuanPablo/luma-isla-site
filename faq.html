About Luma-Isla

Who is Luma-Isla Studios?

We're a veteran-owned, family-run 3D printing workshop based in Edmond, Oklahoma. Our founder is a U.S. military veteran, and the workshop is staffed entirely by family. We run 30+ printers and hand-finish every piece ourselves — nothing is outsourced and nothing is dropshipped.

Why 3D printed instead of cast bronze, ceramic, or resin?

Three reasons buyers tell us they prefer our work:

  • Lighter and shatter-resistant. PETG won't crack like ceramic or chip like resin if it falls.
  • Genuinely affordable. A cast-bronze statue of comparable size and detail can run $300–$1,500. Our bronze-finished pieces deliver the same gallery look at a fraction of that cost.
  • Made by a small American family workshop. Every piece is hand-finished here, not stamped out overseas.
Where do you ship from?

Every order ships from our workshop in Edmond, Oklahoma. We pack and ship in-house — your order does not get handed off to a fulfillment center.

Are you affiliated with any larger company?

No. Luma-Isla Studios is an independent veteran- and family-run small business. We're not a reseller, not a print-on-demand store, and not affiliated with any of the brands or franchises that may inspire our fan-made cosplay or fantasy pieces.

Why "Luma-Isla"?

It reflects what we value about the workshop — light (luma) and a little island of focused craftsmanship in a world of mass-produced decor. We believe small, hand-finished work still matters.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does it take to receive my order?

Most in-stock items ship in 1–3 business days. Once shipped, US delivery typically takes 2–5 business days via USPS or UPS Ground. Custom and personalized pieces add 5–10 business days for hand-finishing.

Your order confirmation email shows real-time status, and Etsy buyers can track everything through their account.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes — free standard shipping on US orders $35 and up. Orders under $35 ship at calculated rates. International shipping is calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship to most countries. International orders typically arrive in 7–18 business days depending on customs in your country. Buyers are responsible for any local duties or import taxes.

Can I get my order by a specific date?

Almost always — message us before ordering with the date and your zip code, and we'll confirm whether standard or expedited shipping will get it to you in time. We've shipped a lot of last-minute Father's Day, First Communion, and birthday gifts; we'll do everything we can.

Is tracking included?

Yes — every order ships with full tracking. You'll receive the tracking number by email the moment your label is printed.

What happens if my package is lost or stuck in transit?

Message us. If a package shows no movement for more than 7 business days, we'll open a carrier trace and either reship the order or issue a full refund — whichever you prefer. We don't make customers fight with the post office.

Returns, Refunds & Damaged Items

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery on non-personalized, non-custom items in original condition. Buyer covers return shipping. Once the item arrives back to us in resalable condition, we issue a full refund within 3 business days.

Personalized, engraved, custom-color, or custom-size pieces are non-returnable unless they arrive damaged or defective.

What if my item arrives damaged?

Message us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage and the packaging. We will either reprint and reship at no cost, or issue a full refund — your choice. We pack carefully, so this is rare, but we stand behind every piece.

How long does a refund take to show up?

Once we receive the returned item (or once we approve a damage claim), refunds are issued within 3 business days. Your bank or card issuer typically posts the refund within another 3–5 business days.

Can I exchange a piece for a different finish or size?

Yes — message us first. Exchanges are easiest if the original piece is still in original condition. For personalized items we usually offer a replacement at our cost rather than a strict exchange.

Materials, Finish & Care

Is the bronze finish actual metal?

No — every piece described as "bronze" is a premium PETG sculpture with a hand-applied bronze-tone finish. This makes it lightweight, shatter-resistant, and dramatically more affordable than cast bronze, while keeping the gallery look. Up close it reads as a hand-finished art piece, not a plastic toy.

What is PETG and is it safe?

PETG is a premium 3D printing material widely used in food-safe containers and medical devices. It's non-toxic, BPA-free, and physically very durable — much tougher than PLA and significantly more heat-resistant. Safe for homes with children and pets as decor (we don't recommend any small piece as a teether, of course). Every piece in our shop is printed in PETG.

Will the bronze finish fade or wear off?

The hand-applied finish is sealed and designed to last indoors for many years. Keep pieces out of direct sunlight to preserve the color over the long term. We have repeat buyers whose first piece from us is still on their shelf five years later.

How do I clean and care for my piece?

Dust gently with a soft, dry brush or microfiber cloth. For deeper cleaning, a barely-damp cloth is fine — avoid soaking, harsh chemicals, alcohol, or abrasive cleaners. For Catholic statues and bronze busts, occasional dusting is all the maintenance you'll ever need.

Are these pieces safe outdoors?

Most pieces are finished for indoor display as standard. PETG is UV- and heat-stable enough for occasional covered porch use, but extended outdoor exposure isn't recommended for our standard bronze-finished sculptures.

Need an outdoor-ready version? We can produce most pieces with an upgraded UV-resistant outdoor sealant for garden, patio, or memorial-marker placement — message us before ordering and we'll quote the outdoor upgrade.

Cosplay Masks & Sets

How do you size a cosplay mask to me?

Send three measurements:

  • Head circumference — measured around the widest part of your head, just above the ears.
  • Ear-to-ear — over the top of your head.
  • Forehead-to-nape — front to back over the crown.

Photos are helpful too. We size most masks to fit adult head circumferences from 22"–23.5" by default; custom sizing adds about 5 business days.

Are the masks comfortable for long convention wear?

Yes — once you complete the quick padding step. Our masks are vented for airflow and weigh under 1 lb, but we intentionally do not pre-install interior padding. Head and face structures vary too much from person to person for one-size-fits-all foam to be comfortable.

Every mask ships with a fitting guide that walks you through it — which specific padding materials to buy (we name products you'll find at any craft, hobby, or hardware store), how to cut them to size, and exactly where to place them inside the mask for your face. Most buyers complete the fit in 10–15 minutes.

Once fitted, the mask is comfortable for full-day convention wear. We've shipped masks worn at every major US convention.

Are your fan-made cosplay pieces officially licensed?

No — every fan-inspired piece (Marvel, LOTR, Black Panther, etc.) is independently designed and sold as an original art piece. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any studio, publisher, or licensor. Pieces are sold as fan-made tribute work and original sculpture.

Meet the Family & Lucien's Tiny Treasures

Who runs Luma-Isla Studios?

Luma-Isla is a true family operation:

  • Mom — keeps the workshop and orders moving. Organizes orders, makes sure every package is packed safely, handwrites the gift notes.
  • Lucien Alessandro — our son. He has his own role here at Luma-Isla — and his own shop. Read on.

That's the whole team. Every piece you order is touched by family hands, never outsourced.

What is Lucien's Tiny Treasures?

Where Play Meets Purpose. Made with Love, Built for Growth.

Lucien's Tiny Treasures is our sister shop, run by our son Lucien Alessandro — with Mom and Dad helping behind the scenes.

It started because Lucien kept watching Dad design, print, and pack orders — and asking, "Can I help?" At first it was small things: handing over packing supplies, picking what we should make next, proudly standing by the door when a package was ready to go out. But it quickly became clear this wasn't just curiosity. He wanted to be part of building something real.

So we turned it into a family project with purpose. This shop isn't about being the biggest or the most profitable. It's about teaching Lucien what growth looks like in real life — how ideas turn into products, how quality matters, how you take care of people, and how consistency adds up over time. We want him to learn that business can be creative, honest, and kind.

Every time you place an order from Lucien's shop, you're not just buying a 3D printed item — you're joining a family story we're building together, and helping a little boy learn confidence, responsibility, and pride in his work.

Where can I find Lucien's Tiny Treasures?

Lucien's shop lives on Etsy: lucienstinytreasures.etsy.com. Stop by, see what tiny treasures he's curated, and leave a kind review if you order — Lucien reads every single one, and Mom reads them with him.

Can I send Lucien a message or note with my order?

Yes — please do. Lucien lights up when buyers leave a note for him. Add anything you'd like to say in the order notes at checkout (on either the Lucien's Tiny Treasures shop or here at Luma-Isla). Mom reads them with him, and they often inspire what gets added to the shop next.

How do you keep it a family workshop as you grow?

Intentionally. Every piece across both shops is hand-finished by us — never outsourced, never dropshipped. We've turned down growth opportunities that would have meant losing that. The day we'd have to choose between scaling fast and keeping the work family-made, we'd choose family. That's the whole point of why we built this in the first place.

Why Our Site Changed

What happened to the Shopify store at luma-isla.com?

We moved! luma-isla.com is now a portfolio site that showcases our work and tells the story of the workshop. All purchases — with current sizes, finishes, reviews from buyers, and full checkout — now happen on our Etsy storefront: lumaisla.etsy.com.

You'll find the same hand-finished pieces, the same family workshop behind every order, and the same handwritten gift notes — just a simpler experience for buyers and a lighter site for us to maintain.

Why did you move off Shopify?

Two honest reasons:

  • Where buyers already are. Most of our orders already came through Etsy. Maintaining two storefronts meant duplicated work — inventory updates, photo edits, listings — on a small family team. Consolidating to Etsy lets us spend that time at the bench instead.
  • What the website should do. luma-isla.com should be a place that tells you who we are and what we make. A site doesn't need to also be a cart and checkout when our Etsy storefront does that job already, and does it well.

If you're a returning Shopify customer, your past orders are still on file — message us if you need anything pulled from your old order history.

Is anything different about the products themselves?

No. Same workshop, same family, same hand-finishing, same materials, same packaging, same free handwritten gift notes. The only change is where you check out.

What about gift cards or store credit from the old Shopify site?

If you have an outstanding gift card or store credit from the previous Shopify storefront, message us at info@luma-isla.com with your order number or the email used on the original order, and we'll honor the balance on your next Etsy purchase.
