About Luma-Isla Studios

We're a veteran-owned, family-run workshop based in Edmond, Oklahoma.

Every piece we ship is made and hand-finished by us, in our own shop, on machines we've built and grown over the last six years. Nothing here is mass-produced, drop-shipped, or outsourced.

This is our family's work, and we treat it that way.

— 2019 · Spare bedroom · One printer
— How this started

One printer in a spare bedroom.

Luma-Isla began in 2019 with one 3D printer in a spare bedroom.

I'm Juan Pablo, a U.S. veteran, and I wanted to build something with my own hands that I could one day pass down to my family. What started as small sculptures for friends slowly grew by word of mouth. One printer became two. Two became five. Eventually, that little spare-bedroom project turned into a full family workshop.

Today, we run 30+ printers out of our shop in Oklahoma. Every piece that leaves here is hand-sanded, primed, painted, bronze-finished, or detailed by hand, then personally inspected by me or someone in my family before it ever goes in the box.

— Our promise

No outsourcing.
No drop-shipping.
No factory line pretending to be handmade.

Every piece is made by our family here in Oklahoma. If something arrives damaged or does not meet your expectations, message us and we'll make it right.

For memorial and gift orders, we ship discreetly with no pricing on the outside of the box. We can also include a free handwritten note if you would like one. We know some of these orders carry a lot of meaning, and we treat them with the same care we would want for something made for our own family.

We believe the things you bring into your home should mean something.

  • A piece you chose.
  • A piece someone made by hand.
  • A piece with a story behind it.

That's what we build here.

Thank you for supporting our small, veteran-owned, family-run American workshop.
